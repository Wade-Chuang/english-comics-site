EP.06

績效面談與升遷

學習目標：專業自我倡議句型

年度績效面談，主管翻開評估表，小莊坐直了身體。

Manager

Overall, your performance this year has been strong.

整體來說，你今年的表現很不錯。

Zhuang

Thank you, I've worked hard to improve my client communication.

謝謝，我一直努力改善跟客戶的溝通。

Manager

I've noticed. Are you interested in taking on more responsibility?

我有注意到。你有興趣承擔更多責任嗎？

Zhuang

Absolutely, I'd welcome the opportunity to lead a project.

當然，我很樂意有機會主導一個專案。

Vocab

"take on more responsibility" = 承擔更多責任，面談常見用語。

Grammar

"I'd welcome the opportunity to..." 展現積極度但不失謙虛的自我倡議句型。

發音 Pronunciation

responsibility 五音節詞，重音在 -BIL-；opportunity 重音在 -TUNE-。
